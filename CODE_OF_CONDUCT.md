# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Morvix adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its code of conduct. The full
text is available here:

- https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: everyone participating in this project - through issues, pull requests,
discussions, or any other channel - is expected to help keep it a welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free environment for people of every background and identity. Be kind,
assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is
best for the community and the project.

Behavior that harasses, demeans, or excludes others is not acceptable and may lead to
removal of contributions, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban, at the maintainer's
discretion.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces and also when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the
maintainer:

- **Krzysztof Adamczyk - krzysiuadamczyk0@gmail.com**

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy and safety will be respected. Enforcement follows the guidelines described in the
Contributor Covenant 2.1 linked above.
